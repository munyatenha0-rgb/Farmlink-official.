Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about buying and selling on FarmLink.

How does delivery work?

FarmLink transporters collect your order from the farm and bring it to a checkpoint near you ($3 flat, collection windows listed at checkout) or to your door ($5 within 10km of Harare CBD, same day for orders before 10am).

What does FarmLink charge?

Buyers pay a $3 checkpoint or $5 home delivery fee plus a 10% service fee on the produce value. Farmers keep 90% of every sale and never pay for transport.

When does the farmer get paid?

Your payment is held by FarmLink and released to the farmer once you confirm delivery — the same escrow model used by large marketplaces.

Where are the pickup checkpoints?

Baba's Tuckshop in Avondale (Tue–Fri, 2–6pm), Mama's Market Stall in Glen Norah (Wed and Sat, 10am–4pm) and Msasa Park Depot (Mon–Sat, 9am–5pm).

I am a farmer — how do I join?

Apply on the Sell on FarmLink page. It is free. We verify your farm, publish your store, and start collecting from you.
