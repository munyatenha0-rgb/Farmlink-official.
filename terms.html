Terms and Conditions

Effective date: 12 September 2026 · Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. The service

FarmLink is an online marketplace. Verified farmers list produce in their own stores; buyers order and pay through FarmLink; FarmLink arranges collection from the farm and delivery to a checkpoint or the buyer's door. FarmLink is the merchant of record for payment handling and passes produce revenue to farmers after delivery is confirmed.

2. Prices and fees

Produce prices are set by farmers and shown per unit. Buyers pay: (a) the produce price; (b) a delivery fee — $3 for checkpoint pickup or $5 for home delivery within 10km of Harare CBD; and (c) a FarmLink service fee of 10% of the produce price. Farmers receive the produce price minus the 10% service fee. Farmers never pay transport costs.

3. Payments

Payment is made by EcoCash, OneMoney or bank transfer at checkout. Funds are held by FarmLink and released to the farmer once the buyer confirms delivery. If produce is unavailable or unacceptably damaged, the produce portion is refunded to the buyer in full.

4. Delivery and checkpoints

Checkpoint orders are available during the collection windows shown at checkout and must be collected by the buyer or their nominee. Home delivery is same-day for orders placed before 10:00 on business days within the delivery zone. FarmLink is responsible for produce from farm collection until handover.

5. Farmer obligations

Farmers must provide accurate farm and produce information, supply orders that match their listings in quantity and quality, and make produce available for collection at the agreed time. Misrepresentation leads to store suspension.

6. Accounts and acceptable use

You must provide accurate information and keep your phone number reachable. You may not use the platform for anything unlawful, misleading or harmful to other users.

7. Liability

Produce is sold by the farmer; FarmLink's role is marketplace, payment agent and transporter. FarmLink's liability is limited to the value of the affected order. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

8. Changes and law

We may update these terms; the current version on this page applies to new orders. These terms are governed by the laws of Zimbabwe.
