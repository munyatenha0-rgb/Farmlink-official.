About FarmLink

Founded in Harare in 2026, FarmLink exists for one reason: the farmer who grows the food should keep most of the money it sells for.

Our story

FarmLink started with a simple observation on the Mazowe road: farmers were selling crates of tomatoes at the farm gate for a fraction of what those same tomatoes cost in Harare — and spending their own money and a full day getting the rest to market. We built FarmLink to close that gap: every farmer gets a free online store, every buyer gets produce delivered to a checkpoint near them, and FarmLink handles the transport and the payment in between. Farmers keep 90% of every sale. That model is working — see the pilot results.
