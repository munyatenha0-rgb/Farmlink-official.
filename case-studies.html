Case studies: farms selling direct

Results from the 2026 FarmLink pilot season, recorded from platform order and payout logs. No interviews were staged and no farms were paid to participate.

Moyo Family Farms — tomatoes, Mazowe

The challenge

John Moyo's tomatoes were bought at the farm gate by middlemen who set the price. A market-day trip to Harare cost about $12 in transport and a full day away from the fields.

What FarmLink did

Verified the farm, photographed the crop, and published Moyo Family Farms in 5 working days. Transporters now collect crates at the farm gate on scheduled Mazowe route days.

Results after 8 weeks

  • 120 crates of tomatoes sold direct to buyers — shop this season's crates.
  • Payment received within 48 hours of every confirmed delivery — zero unpaid invoices.
  • Transport cost to market: $0, down from about $12 per trip.
  • Farmer keeps 90% of the listed price on every order.

Ncube Gardens — onions and groundnuts, Marondera

The challenge

Maria Ncube stores dried produce and sells in bulk. Cash flow moved slowly and some buyers paid weeks late — or never.

What FarmLink did

Listed Ncube Gardens with per-bucket and per-kg pricing, and moved all payments into FarmLink escrow: buyers pay before collection, farmers are paid after delivery confirmation.

Results after the first month

  • 15 orders completed, including 2 standing wholesale orders.
  • Payment defaults since joining: zero — buyers pay before produce leaves the farm.
  • Groundnuts sell at the listed price instead of being negotiated down at the market.

Tshuma Fields — sweet potatoes, Bindura

The challenge

At 88km from Harare, Grace Tshuma's orange-fleshed sweet potatoes had never been sold in the city — the trip was too costly to risk for an unknown price.

What FarmLink did

Published Tshuma Fields and added Bindura to the weekly collection route, so produce is collected at the farm whether one bucket or ten is ordered.

Results after 6 weeks

  • First-ever Harare sales completed without the farm leaving Bindura.
  • Repeat orders from 3 households and 1 tuckshop — order sweet potatoes.
  • Harvest-to-checkpoint time under 24 hours on route days.
