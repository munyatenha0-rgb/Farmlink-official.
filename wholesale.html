Wholesale produce supply

For tuckshops, retailers, schools, restaurants and kitchens that need reliable weekly produce without driving to the market.

1

Standing weekly order

Tell us what you need each week — for example 10 crates of tomatoes, 5 buckets of onions, 20 bunches of rape. Your order is reserved on the farm before harvest day.

2

Route-day delivery

Everything arrives together on your district's route day, at your door or nearest checkpoint, in ventilated crates.

3

One monthly invoice

Pay by bank transfer (FBC, Steward, CBZ) on monthly invoicing, or per order by EcoCash or OneMoney. Payment is due after delivery, not before.

What you get

  • Volume pricing agreed per farm, locked for the season.
  • Minimum order: 5 crates (or equivalent) per delivery.
  • Substitutions agreed in advance if a farm's harvest falls short.
  • Quality checked at farm collection — shortfalls or damage credited on the next invoice.
  • A named contact who responds within 1 business day.

Get a wholesale quote

Tell us your weekly quantities and delivery point. We reply within 1 business day with per-farm pricing.
