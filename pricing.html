Simple, honest pricing

No subscriptions, no listing fees, no hidden charges. FarmLink earns only when produce sells.

For buyers

ChargeAmountWhat it covers
ProduceSet by each farmerThe price you see per crate, bucket, bunch or kg goes to the farmer (minus the service fee below).
Checkpoint pickup$3 flat per orderCollection from the farm and delivery to your chosen checkpoint (Avondale, Glen Norah or Msasa Park).
Home delivery$5 per orderDoor-to-door delivery within 10km of Harare CBD. Same day for orders placed before 10am.
FarmLink service fee10% of produce valuePayment handling, farm verification, order support and the escrow that protects your money until delivery is confirmed.

Worked example

2 crates of tomatoes at $13 each$26
Checkpoint pickup (Avondale)$3
FarmLink service fee (10% of $26)$3
You pay$32

The farmer receives $23 and is paid within 48 hours of your delivery confirmation. The transporter receives the $3 delivery fee.
