Farm collection and delivery logistics

FarmLink's own transporters collect from farms and deliver to checkpoints across Harare. Farmers never pay for transport; buyers pay a flat $3 checkpoint or $5 home delivery fee (see pricing).

How collection works

1

Order cutoff

Orders placed before 10am enter the same day's route. Later orders go out next business day.

2

Farm collection

Transporters collect packed produce from the farm in ventilated crates. Produce is checked against the order before it leaves.

3

Checkpoint or door

Produce moves to your chosen checkpoint for collection in its published window, or straight to your door within the delivery zone.
