Rape (covo): five weeknight ways beyond plain greens

23 July 2026 · FarmLink Team · 4 min read

Rape is one of Zimbabwe's cheapest and most nutritious vegetables — iron, vitamin A, fibre — and it is on almost every FarmLink order. If it has become the same boiled side on repeat, these five methods take under 20 minutes each.

1. The 10-minute bright rape

High heat, a splash of oil, crushed garlic, torn leaves, salt. Stir-fry until just wilted and still bright. Overcooking is what turns rape army-green and bitter.

2. Tomato and onion braise

Fry chopped onion until sweet, add grated tomato, simmer five minutes, then fold in chopped rape stalks first (2 minutes) and leaves (3 minutes). The stalks need the head start.

3. Peanut butter greens

Simmer rape in a little water, then stir in a spoon of peanut butter off the heat until silky. Pairs perfectly with white maize sadza and adds protein to the plate.

4. Rape and egg scramble

Wilt chopped rape in a pan, pour over beaten eggs, and finish as a scramble. An inexpensive, fast breakfast that uses yesterday's bunch.

5. Beans and greens stew

Combine a tin or pot of cooked sugar beans with tomato, onion and chopped rape; simmer 10 minutes. A complete, meat-free main for a family at a few dollars.
